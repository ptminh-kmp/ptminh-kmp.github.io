Match-3 is probably the most familiar puzzle shape on a phone: swap two pieces, line up three, watch them pop. I didn't want to reinvent it. I wanted a version that felt *warm* — pieces with faces, a board that changes with the seasons, and levels that ask you to think about a move instead of just spamming swaps.

That became **Jellora: Jelly Match Puzzle**.

---

## Small Jellies, Thoughtful Moves

Swap colorful jellies, line up three or more, and clear each level's goals before your moves run out. Goals change from level to level: break **crates**, melt **ice**, release **chained jellies**, all on board shapes that keep shifting as you progress. Every tenth level is a harder challenge, so the difficulty curve has a rhythm instead of a straight ramp.

## Big Combos

Match more than three and you get something better than a clear: **striped jellies**, **wrapped bombs**, **fish** and **rainbow** boosters. Combine two special jellies for a bigger effect. For a tricky level, you can bring a few special jellies from your inventory onto the board before the first move, and **hammers**, **magic hands** and **shuffles** give you ways out when the board gets stuck.

## 2,000 Levels, 20 Chapters

A colorful level map carries you through changing scenery, with stars on every level you've cleared so you can see at a glance where there's still room to improve. Build a winning streak and you earn extra tools and special jellies along the way.

## Make The Board Yours

Clearing levels earns in-game coins, and coins unlock themes in the shop: winter snow, Christmas lights, Lunar New Year decorations, spring blossoms, ocean colors, autumn leaves and Halloween pumpkins. Switch between unlocked themes whenever you like. Themes are bought with in-game coins only; there is no real-money checkout.

## A Lively Little World

Jellies blink, smile and bounce. If you pause to think, an animated hint nudges you toward a move, and a waving jelly crew greets you on launch. Music, sound effects, **reduced motion** and a **colorblind mode** are all adjustable in Settings.

## Play Your Way

Start as a guest, no account needed. If you want your progress on more than one device, sign in with Google, Apple or email. The interface speaks 10 languages, including English and Vietnamese, switchable anytime.

## Why I Built It This Way

I wanted a puzzle game I'd be happy to open for five minutes at the end of the day: cheerful without being noisy, challenging without being punishing, and with enough variety in goals, combos and themes that level 300 still feels different from level 3.
