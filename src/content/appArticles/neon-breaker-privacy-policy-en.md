**Effective date:** August 31, 2026

**Overview**
Brick Breaker: Neon ("the app") is a brick-breaking arcade game. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control or delete it.

**Data we collect**
Account data (only if you choose to sign in):
  • Guest sign-in: an anonymous account identifier and the display name you type in. No email or password.
  • Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple: your account's user ID, email address, and display name, provided to us by Google or Apple when you sign in.

Gameplay data: your score, stars, and level progress, submitted to a public leaderboard under your display name and stored against your account identifier.

Local-only data: your lives, unlocked levels, and app settings (language, sound, music, haptics) are stored on your device via local preferences. Progress also syncs to your account when you're signed in.

Technical data: non-identifiable device and session information (device model, OS version, session length, crash reports) collected via Google Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics to improve stability and fix bugs.

Advertising data: the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use advertising identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA, requested via the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS, or Android's advertising ID) to show relevant ads and to power the optional rewarded-ad bonus life. If you decline tracking permission, or on Android generally, ads are shown without personalization.

**How we use this data**
To let you save progress and compete on personal and global leaderboards, to show and measure ads that support free access to the app (including the optional rewarded bonus life), and to diagnose crashes and performance issues. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for any purpose other than the above.

**Third parties**
Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) stores your account, progress, and leaderboard data, and powers app diagnostics. Google AdMob, Google Sign-In, and Sign in with Apple serve ads and (optionally) sign you in. These are covered by Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and Apple's Privacy Policy respectively.

**Data retention & deletion**
Account, progress, and leaderboard data is kept until you delete it. You can permanently delete your account from Settings → Delete Account at any time. This removes your account and its associated progress and leaderboard profile. Individual past leaderboard scores may remain visible as historical entries tied to a now-deleted account identifier, with no way to link them back to you afterward.

**Children's privacy**
Brick Breaker: Neon is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us below and we will delete it.

**Your choices**
You can play without signing in at all; sign-in is only required to appear on leaderboards. You can sign out or delete your account at any time from Settings, and switch the app's language independently of your device settings. On iOS, you can change your ad tracking permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

**Changes to this policy**
We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
